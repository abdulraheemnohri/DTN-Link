elevation). Predictor::passes_between refines the horizon crossings by
bisection into non-overlapping unix-second windows, and
to_contact_plan(addr, from, hours) turns them into a ContactPlan the
SatelliteGateway runs unchanged. CLI: dtn-link tle passes|plan.
Honest scope: a mean-element model, not SGP4 (no B* drag, no J2
node regression; a sub-15-second pass can be missed); TLE files are
fetched by the operator.
